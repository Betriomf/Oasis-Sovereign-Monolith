\noindent\textbf{Executive Overview:}
We present an open-access, formally verified computational pipeline (\emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline}, OVRP) modeling coarse-grained protein folding kinetics, non-Markovian stress plateaus, and non-equilibrium ATP chaperone rescue. All algebraic invariants are machine-checked in \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} (\texttt{0~sorry}) alongside deterministic \texttt{C99} numerical probes and Bitcoin OpenTimestamps provenance. Released under copyleft commons (\texttt{CC-BY-SA-4.0} / \texttt{AGPL-3.0-or-later}) for unrestricted academic and public-health reuse.
