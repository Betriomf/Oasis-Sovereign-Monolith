\begin{abstract}
Within the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}), we conduct a comparative computational and formal audit linking two-qubit entanglement entropy, discrete parity/midpoint symmetries, and open foundational problems in gauge theory, string unification, and analytic number theory. In \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} (\texttt{EntanglementSymmetry\_Spec.lean}, \texttt{0 sorry}, \texttt{0} custom axioms), we formally verify exact finite conservation and discrete reflection lemmas. In \texttt{C99}, we simulate reduced-density-matrix von Neumann entropy $S(\rho_A) = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_A \ln \rho_A)$ across noisy two-qubit states alongside finite discrete parity-balance probes, explicitly separating finite verified computations from open problems in continuous Yang--Mills confinement, string unification, and the Riemann Hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Comparative Audit Scope}
Quantum entanglement captures non-classical correlations across subsystems, whereas non-Abelian gauge lattices study confinement and spectral gaps, and analytic number theory examines prime distributions and zeta zeros. Rather than conflating distinct mathematical regimes, OVRP audits finite invariants mechanically while recording open analytic frontiers explicitly:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Formal Layer (\texttt{Lean~4.36.0}):} Zero-\texttt{sorry} proofs of finite probability-weight normalization and discrete reflection symmetry.
  \item \textbf{Numerical Probe (\texttt{C99}):} Evaluation of reduced von Neumann entropy $S(\rho_A)$ for mixed/pure two-qubit ensembles and finite discrete balance metrics.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Explicit Open Boundaries]
Finite two-qubit entropy evaluations and discrete parity probes do not establish continuum Yang--Mills confinement, full string-theoretic unification, or the Riemann Hypothesis.
\end{remark}
