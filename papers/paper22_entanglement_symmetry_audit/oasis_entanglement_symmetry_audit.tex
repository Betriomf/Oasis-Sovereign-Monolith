\begin{abstract}
Within the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}), we present a comparative computational and formal audit examining two-qubit reduced von Neumann entropy $S(\rho_A) = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_A \ln \rho_A)$, discrete midpoint/parity symmetries in \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} (\texttt{EntanglementSymmetry\_Spec.lean}, \texttt{0 sorry}, \texttt{0} custom axioms), and open problems across gauge theory, string unification, and analytic number theory. We document both an initial internal consistency table and a contrast table comparing a pure Bell state ($S(\rho_A) = \ln 2$) against a separable product state ($S(\rho_A) = 0$) and noisy diagonal states in \texttt{C99}, together with conclusions and an open-commons licensing stance.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusions and Open Licensing Stance}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Table~1 Conclusion:} Identical evaluations across $w \in [0, 1]$ with $\Delta S = 0$ confirm numerical consistency of the binary von Neumann entropy routine in \texttt{C99} without hidden drift.
  \item \textbf{Table~2 Conclusion:} Comparing a pure Bell state ($S(\rho_A) = \ln 2 \approx 0.693147$) against a separable product state ($S(\rho_A) = 0$) and a noisy diagonal state clarifies how subsystem entropy separates maximal single-qubit entanglement from separable/noisy baselines in finite dimension.
  \item \textbf{Open Commons Licensing:} Following OVRP transparency standards, verification artifacts and code are released under open, non-restrictive commons terms (such as \texttt{Apache-2.0} or \texttt{CC-BY-4.0}) so researchers and engineers can audit, reproduce, and build upon them openly.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Explicit Open Boundaries]

\end{remark}
