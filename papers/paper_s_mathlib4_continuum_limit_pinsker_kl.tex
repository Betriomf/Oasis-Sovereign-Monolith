\begin{abstract}
Building directly upon the finite-volume $SU(3)$ gauge and Wilson measure formalization in \texttt{Mathlib4} (\texttt{OasisMathlib}, DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.24370265}), we present the analytical and measure-theoretic continuum bridge modules (\texttt{Q5}--\texttt{Q8}): geometric Cauchy convergence of dyadic characteristic sequences in $\mathbb{C}$ via \texttt{cauchySeq\_of\_le\_geometric} (\texttt{ThermodynamicContinuumLimit.lean}); canonical spectral gap synthesis from $\mathrm{Ric}_{SU(3)}$ and Log-Sobolev dissipation (\texttt{YangMills\_Millennium\_Inconditional.lean}); deduction of $2^{-n}$ dyadic step bounds from $4^{-n}$ relative-entropy decay (\texttt{PinskerRenormalizationStep.lean}); coupling to \texttt{Mathlib4} probability measures and Radon--Nikodym $L^1$ jumps (\texttt{KullbackLeibler\_PinskerBridge.lean}); and two-point Kullback--Leibler non-negativity and differential convexity bounds (\texttt{ElementalPinsker\_Inequality.lean}).
\end{abstract}
