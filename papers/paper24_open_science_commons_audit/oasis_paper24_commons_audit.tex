\begin{abstract}
Continuing from \emph{Paper~23~bis} (cross-domain synthesis across entanglement, thermodynamics, information geometry, gauge/string contexts, zeta symmetries, and discrete dynamics) under the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}), this companion paper (\texttt{Paper~24}) examines how verifiable auditability, low-overhead reproducibility, and open commons licensing support scientific integrity and public-interest engineering. Alongside $\ln 2 \approx 0.693147$, $\ln \phi \approx 0.481212$, and $\ln 10 \approx 2.302585$ as a recurring probe reference/attractor, we provide a zero-\texttt{sorry} \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} specification (\texttt{Paper24\_CommonsAudit\_Spec.lean}) and a deterministic \texttt{C99} resource-allocation and efficiency probe sealed with \texttt{SHA-256} and Bitcoin OpenTimestamps.
\end{abstract}

\section{From Cross-Domain Synthesis to Open Commons Audit}
While \emph{Paper~23~bis} maps observables and open frontiers across physics and discrete mathematics, \emph{Paper~24} focuses on practical scientific and engineering stewardship:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Verifiable Reproducibility:} Pairing machine-checked finite lemmas in \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} (\texttt{0 sorry}, \texttt{0} custom axioms) with deterministic \texttt{C99} probes and timestamped \texttt{SHA-256} manifests.
  \item \textbf{Common-Good Licensing:} Publishing methods and verification artifacts under permissive open licenses (\texttt{CC-BY-4.0} and \texttt{Apache-2.0}) so independent researchers, educators, and builders can inspect and reuse them.
  \item \textbf{Honest Scope Boundaries:} Keeping verified finite checks distinct from unresolved foundational conjectures in physics and mathematics.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Explicit Scope and Open Boundaries]
Verifiable open-commons methodology improves auditability and reuse, but does not substitute for proofs of open foundational problems in physics or mathematics.
\end{remark}
