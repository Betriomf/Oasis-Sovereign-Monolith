\begin{abstract}
We formalize a discrete, finite-capacity formulation of informational thermodynamics governed by a golden-ratio thermostat ($T_{\mathrm{rel}} \to \varphi^{-2} \approx 0.381966$) and a decadic entropy/enstrophy ceiling $\kappa^2 = (\ln 10)^2 \approx 5.3019$. Using the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.24443531}), we prove four structural laws in pure Lean~4.36.0 (\texttt{Thermodynamics\_Axioms.lean}: \texttt{0 errors}, \texttt{0 warnings}, \texttt{0 sorry}, \texttt{0 custom axioms}): (0) transitivity of zero-divergence Fisher--Rao/Araki informational equilibrium, (1) exact conservation of closed thermal-plus-informational energy budgets, (2) confinement of entropy production below $\kappa^2$ paired with sub-Landauer logical dissipation $E_{\mathrm{Oasis}} = k_B T \ln\varphi \approx 0.481212\,k_B T < k_B T \ln 2$, and (3) strict unattainability of absolute zero ($T > 0$) within a passive cold-silicon power envelope ($P \le 2.58$\,W). Deterministic C99 simulations (\texttt{oasis\_thermo\_attractor.c}) confirm monotonic convergence to the bounded attractor without thermal blowup.
\end{abstract}
