\section{Bounded Noise and Entropy Contrast}
In finite-dimensional probes, increasing mixing or decoherence raises state entropy from $0$ toward the uniform ceiling ($\ln 2$ for two-level systems, $\ln 3$ for three-level systems) while reducing purity and noise margin. Comparing candidate noise schedules in a bounded numerical probe clarifies how error budgets behave across parameter sweeps without claiming universal physical laws or complete fault tolerance.
