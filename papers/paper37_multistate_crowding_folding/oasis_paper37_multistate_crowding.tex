\section{Two-Stage Molten-Globule Kinetics and Crowding Trade-Off}
For multi-state proteins traversing a molten-globule intermediate $I_{\mathrm{MG}}$ (\texttt{arXiv:2204.05771}), total relaxation decomposes into hydrophobic collapse ($U \to I_{\mathrm{MG}}$) and native packing ($I_{\mathrm{MG}} \to N$). Under intracellular volume fraction $\phi_{\mathrm{crowd}}$, excluded-volume compaction accelerates folding by $\exp(\alpha\,\phi_{\mathrm{crowd}})$ while cytoplasmic viscosity dampens chain diffusion by $\exp(-\beta\,\phi_{\mathrm{crowd}}^2)$:
\begin{equation}
k_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathrm{RCO}, \phi_{\mathrm{crowd}}) = k_{\mathrm{fold}}(\mathrm{RCO})\,\exp\bigl(\alpha\,\phi_{\mathrm{crowd}} - \beta\,\phi_{\mathrm{crowd}}^2\bigr), \qquad \alpha = 1.8,\;\beta = 4.5.
\end{equation}
In the deterministic \texttt{C99} probe, $k_{\mathrm{eff}}$ peaks near $\phi_{\mathrm{opt}} = \alpha/(2\beta) = 0.20$ (matching physiological macromolecular volume fractions), reducing total folding steps for Barnase (\texttt{1BNI}, $\mathrm{RCO} = 0.172$) from 16 steps in dilute buffer ($\phi = 0$) to 13 steps at $\phi = 0.20$, before slowing at high crowding ($\phi = 0.35$).
