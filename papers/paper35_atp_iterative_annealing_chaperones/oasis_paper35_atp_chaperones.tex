\section{Discrete ATP Budget and Iterative Annealing Cycles}
Following \texttt{arXiv:2506.12645} and \texttt{arXiv:1908.11323}, let each chaperone remodeling cycle consume $c_{\mathrm{ATP}} > 0$ units from a bounded cellular ATP budget $E_{\mathrm{ATP}}(0)$, supplying free energy to eject a high-$\mathrm{RCO}$ chain from a kinetically trapped state ($\Delta_{\mathrm{trap}}$) back into a productive folding funnel:
\begin{equation}
E_{\mathrm{ATP}}(t+1) = E_{\mathrm{ATP}}(t) - c_{\mathrm{ATP}}\,\mathbb{I}_{\mathrm{cycle}}(t), \qquad E_{\mathrm{ATP}}(t) \ge 0.
\end{equation}
In the deterministic \texttt{C99} probe calibrated to \texttt{1APS} ($\mathrm{RCO} = 0.236$), passive folding stalls in a kinetic trap ($\Delta_{\mathrm{passive}} \approx 0.280$), whereas three ATP-driven annealing pulses ($3 \times 15\,k_B T = 45\,k_B T$) rescue the trajectory and achieve native threshold closure ($\Delta \le 0.015$) at step~21.
