\begin{abstract}
We present the global scheme closure module (\texttt{GlobalScheme\_Closure.lean}) in pure Lean~4.36.0 (\texttt{0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 sorry, 0 custom axioms}), synthesizing calibrated uniform lattice bounds (\texttt{UniformLatticeBounds\_Fase1.lean}), Sobolev moment and UV/IR difference bounds (\texttt{TightnessMomentBounds\_Fase3.lean}), telescopic step sums (\texttt{tightness\_telescopiccauchy\_fase3.lean}), and relative-entropy / non-triviality separation against a zero-cumulant Gaussian baseline (\texttt{NonTrivialityGap\_Fase4.lean}). All bounds are expressed in scaled integer units calibrated against C99 lattice benchmarks. We explicitly note that this formalization verifies structural bound preservation and scheme consistency rather than an unconditional continuum existence or mass-gap proof in $\mathbb{R}^4$.
\end{abstract}
