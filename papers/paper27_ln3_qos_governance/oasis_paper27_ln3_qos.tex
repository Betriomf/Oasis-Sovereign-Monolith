\section{Ternary Scale Ratios and Quantum-Classical OS Framing}
For a symmetric three-level mixture parameterized by $\varepsilon \in [0, 1]$, entropy spans $[0, \ln 3]$, with $\ln 3 / \ln 2 \approx 1.584963$ and $\ln 3 / \ln 10 \approx 0.477121$. Within a Q-OS control layer, treating $\ln 3$ as a bounded three-state entropy ceiling alongside binary and reference scales helps structure budget checks and ZNE extrapolation experiments as testable engineering hypotheses rather than universal physical laws.

\begin{remark}[Explicit Open Boundaries]
Bounded finite-state entropy probes, ratio comparisons, and ZNE control framing do not establish universal quantum laws, guarantee noise-free qutrit execution, or resolve open mathematical and physical conjectures.
\end{remark}
