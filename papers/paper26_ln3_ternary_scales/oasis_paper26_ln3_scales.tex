\begin{abstract}
Building on the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}) and the critical-line stability note (\texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24539709}), this paper examines $\ln 3 \approx 1.098612\text{ nats}$ as the maximal entropy scale of a uniform three-level (qutrit) state and cross-compares it against $\ln 2 \approx 0.693147\text{ nats}$ and $\ln 10 \approx 2.302585\text{ nats}$ (treated as an observed probe reference/attractor). Deterministic \texttt{C99} sweeps across mixing parameter $\varepsilon \in [0, 1]$, a zero-\texttt{sorry} \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} specification (\texttt{Paper26\_Ln3Scales\_Spec.lean}), and \texttt{SHA-256} + Bitcoin OpenTimestamps provenance accompany the study under explicit open boundaries.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusions and Numerical Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Three-Level Entropy Ceiling:} Along a diagonal three-level mixture parameterized by $\varepsilon \in [0, 1]$, von Neumann entropy rises from $0$ at $\varepsilon = 0.00$ to $S(0.50) \approx 0.867563\text{ nats}$ and reaches $\ln 3 \approx 1.098612\text{ nats}$ at $\varepsilon = 1.00$.
  \item \textbf{Cross-Scale Ratios with $\ln 2$ and $\ln 10$:} At $\varepsilon = 1.00$, $S / \ln 2 \approx 1.584963$, $S / \ln 10 = \ln 3 / \ln 10 \approx 0.477121$, and the additive gap $\ln 10 - \ln 3 \approx 1.203973\text{ nats}$ remains positive.
  \item \textbf{Scope and Open Boundaries:} These results characterize finite three-level mixture probes and logarithmic scale ratios; they do not establish universal quantum laws or resolve open foundational conjectures.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Explicit Open Boundaries]
Numerical ratios and finite three-state entropy probes do not prove universal physical laws or close open mathematical and physical problems.
\end{remark}
