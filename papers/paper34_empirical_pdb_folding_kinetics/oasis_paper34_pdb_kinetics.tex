\begin{abstract}
Continuing the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}), bounded informational folding (\texttt{Paper~32}, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24590134}), and non-Markovian stress damping (\texttt{Paper~33}, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24590834}), \texttt{Paper~34} calibrates discrete relaxation rates against published two-state protein folding kinetics from the Protein Data Bank (PDB). Grounded in network-pathway correlations (\texttt{arXiv:2604.04677}), reaction-coordinate non-Markovian friction (\texttt{arXiv:2607.20504}), solvent cooperativity (\texttt{arXiv:2503.23280}), high-contact-order acylphosphatase landscapes (\texttt{arXiv:2203.05786}), and barnase calorimetric force spectroscopy (\texttt{arXiv:2204.05771}), the study maps Relative Contact Order ($\mathrm{RCO}$) across eight benchmark PDB structures (\texttt{1VII}, \texttt{1ENH}, \texttt{2CI2}, \texttt{1UBQ}, \texttt{2PTL}, \texttt{1SHG}, \texttt{1FKB}, \texttt{1APS}) to discrete contraction steps. Verified in \texttt{Lean~4.36.0} (\texttt{0~sorry}) and probed in \texttt{C99}, confirming a strong inverse correlation ($r \approx -0.94$) between discrete step count and experimental $\ln k_f$, released under \texttt{CC-BY-SA-4.0} and \texttt{AGPL-3.0-or-later}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical Clarification and Topological Contact-Order Mapping}
In macroscopic thermal dispersion (such as boiling water atomized into sub-zero air), rapid freezing arises from surface-area amplification and convective/evaporative heat loss rather than a violation of energy balance. Conversely, in aqueous two-state protein folding (\texttt{arXiv:2503.23280}, \texttt{arXiv:2604.04677}), experimental folding rates $\ln k_f$ are governed by native topological complexity measured via Relative Contact Order ($\mathrm{RCO}$). Projecting high-dimensional conformational dynamics onto a 1D reaction coordinate (\texttt{arXiv:2607.20504}), we parameterize the discrete contraction rate as:
\begin{equation}
k_{\mathrm{fold}}(\mathrm{RCO}) = k_0 \exp(-\gamma\,\mathrm{RCO}), \qquad k_0 = 0.95,\;\gamma = 6.2.
\end{equation}

\section{Empirical Benchmark Across Eight Two-State PDB Structures}
Evaluating $\Delta(t+1) = \Delta(t)(1 - k_{\mathrm{fold}}(\mathrm{RCO}))$ from $\Delta(0) = 1.60$ to $\varepsilon = 0.015$ yields discrete step counts $T_{\mathrm{fold}}$ matching experimental $\ln k_f$ ordering across fast helical folds (\texttt{1VII}: $T=4$, $\ln k_f = 11.8$) through slow high-$\mathrm{RCO}$ folds (\texttt{1APS}: $T=15$, $\ln k_f = -1.5$, \texttt{arXiv:2203.05786}).
