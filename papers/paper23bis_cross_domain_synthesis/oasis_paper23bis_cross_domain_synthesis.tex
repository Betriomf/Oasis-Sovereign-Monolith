\begin{abstract}
Building on the \emph{Oasis Verifiable Research Pipeline} (OVRP, \texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24443531}) and the logarithmic scale audit (\texttt{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24538777}), this companion note (\texttt{Paper~23~bis}) surveys how two-qubit entanglement entropy, information thermodynamics, information geometry, entropic gravity, non-Abelian gauge and string contexts, discrete prime/zeta symmetry probes, and Collatz-like discrete iterations fit into a unified audit table. \section{Conclusions and Open Boundaries}
Grouping these domains into a single transparent audit preserves ambitious cross-disciplinary exploration while preventing overclaims. Observing $\ln 10$ as a recurring reference/attractor in our probes motivates further numerical and formal checks, while continuum Yang--Mills confinement, full string unification, entropic quantum gravity, the Riemann Hypothesis, and the general Collatz conjecture remain open.

\begin{remark}[Explicit Open Boundaries]
Cross-domain synthesis and finite probes do not constitute proofs of open Millennium or foundational physics problems.
\end{remark}
