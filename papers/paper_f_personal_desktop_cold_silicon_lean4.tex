Extending our machine-checked Lean 4 verification series (DOIs: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24311596}{10.5281/zenodo.24311596}, \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24323807}{10.5281/zenodo.24323807}, \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24324622}{10.5281/zenodo.24324622}, and \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24324683}{10.5281/zenodo.24324683}), we formalize the \textbf{Cold-Silicon Personal Desktop Architecture}. Memory and context invariants are verified in Lean 4 (\texttt{PersonalDesktop\_ColdSilicon\_Fase12.lean}) with zero unproven axioms.
\end{abstract}

\section{Zero-Swap Memory Budgeting and Thermal Containment}

Restricting execution to a single active quantized slot ($1.5\mathrm{B}$ at $Q4\_K\_M$, $\approx 1200\,\mathrm{MiB}$) with automatic eviction (\texttt{KEEP\_ALIVE=3m}) and two physical threads eliminates thermal throttling (\texttt{Pages throttled = 0}) while preserving over $4\,\mathrm{GiB}$ of headroom for interactive desktop workflows.
