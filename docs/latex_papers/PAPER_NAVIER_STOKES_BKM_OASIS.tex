\begin{abstract}
We present a numerical investigation of the global smoothness for the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes equations subject to periodic boundary conditions. By tracking the time-integrated vorticity norm in the Beale-Kato-Majda (BKM) blow-up criterion, $\int_0^T \|\omega(\cdot, t)\|_{L^\infty} dt < \infty$, we evaluate the non-blowup condition under deterministic Taylor-Green initial configurations. The computational pipeline operates strictly within energy-constrained execution bounds ($\le 3.45$W).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Theoretical Framework}
The 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes system is governed by:
\begin{equation}
\partial_t u + (u \cdot \nabla)u = -\nabla p + \nu \Delta u, \quad \nabla \cdot u = 0
\end{equation}
The classic Beale-Kato-Majda (BKM) theorem establishes that a smooth solution exists on $[0, T]$ if and only if:
\begin{equation}
\int_0^T \|\omega(\cdot, t)\|_{L^\infty} dt < \infty
\end{equation}
where $\omega = \nabla \times u$ denotes the vorticity field.
